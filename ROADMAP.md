# Chicago Corners roadmap

Civic first. Game later. Money last.

## Now (done)
- Map of high-score Chicago corners from open crash data
- Score = how many crashes + how serious (no “who was hit”)
- Treatment list with cost band, fit, and research range
- Place a change on the streets you see, not only on the data pin
- Public page: https://galawolf.github.io/chicago-corners/

## Phase 1 — Make it usable for a neighbor (2–4 weeks)
Goal: someone who lives near a bad corner can find it and talk about one change.

- Search box (street name or two streets)
- Better corner names (two real street names, not a grid guess)
- Zoom to a community area / ward
- One-page “how to read this” in plain language
- Print / share link for a single corner + one placed change

Skip: traffic simulation, logins, payments.

## Phase 2 — Make the sketch honest (1–2 months)
Goal: the picture and the numbers don’t overclaim.

- Footprint rules: roundabout needs space; LPI needs a signal
- Show “fits / maybe / won’t fit” before they drop it
- Turn the research range into a simple “injuries might drop some / a lot / unknown”
- Note data limits on every share card (2023–present, intersection-related only)

Still not an engineering study. Say that every time.

## Phase 3 — Useful to a community group
Goal: a block club or alderman’s office can use it in a meeting.

- Ward or neighborhood filter
- “Top 10 corners in this area”
- Short list of cheap vs expensive changes
- Export one corner as a PDF one-pager
- Optional: resident notes that are clearly unofficial

## Phase 4 — Light game layer (only after Phase 3)
- Budget chip
- Place 1–3 changes, sketch-only score change
- Fail states for fit and wrong tool
- Keep real crash numbers visible

## Phase 5 — Money (later)
Neighborhood packets, workshops, or another city’s open data. No ads on crash maps.
